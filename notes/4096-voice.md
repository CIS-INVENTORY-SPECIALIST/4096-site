# THE 4096 VOICE

A reference for posts, replies, DMs, site copy, and anything else that goes
out with 4096 on it. Written from lines that already worked, not invented.

---

## THE ONE RULE

**State the fact. Accept the outcome. Don't argue for it.**

Everything else in this document is a consequence of that.

---

## WHERE IT COMES FROM

You have been building onchain every day since February and still feel like
an outsider trying to get in. Do not try to sound like an insider. It will
read as costume and the people who are actually inside will notice first.

The outsider position is not a weakness to hide. It is the honest place you
are writing from, and it happens to be the same position 4096 puts everyone
in: nobody gets to see the finished thing, nobody gets to decide, including
the person who built it. You made a work about being outside its own outcome.
Write from outside.

---

## LINES THAT WORKED

These are yours. They are the reference, not a style guide someone else wrote.

> i genuinely can't see it

> this fills in or it doesn't

> i wrote the rules and then stopped

> that's not a delay, it's the reading

> nobody has seen it finished. it isn't finished

> a few of you were in before i'd even posted the thread. that one stays with me

> i wrote that rule and now i get to watch it happen

**What they share.** Short declaratives. No adjective doing persuasive work.
Willing to state a fact that isn't flattering. A flat acceptance of not being
in control.

---

## MECHANICS

**Lowercase.** Always, except proper nouns and the start of a plate caption.

**No em dashes.** Full stop or a new line instead.

**Short lines, hard breaks.** Line breaks do the work commas would.

**Numbers plain.** "176 minted, two sales" not "we're seeing early traction."
The number is the sentence.

**End on the noun.** "this fills in or it doesn't" not "so we'll see how it
fills in over time."

**One idea per post.** If there are two, that is two posts.

---

## WHAT THE VOICE NEVER DOES

**Never argues for its own significance.** Say what happened. If it matters,
that will be obvious. If it doesn't, saying so louder won't help.

**Never asks for engagement.** No "let me know what you think," no "would love
your thoughts," no "drop a comment." If someone wants to reply they will.

**Never explains a decision nobody questioned.** "I'd rather it be real before
I announce anything" is a defence against a charge nobody made. Cut it.

**Never hedges.** "kind of", "sort of", "I think maybe", "just wanted to".
All out.

**Never spins a number.** 176 minted is 176 minted. Two sales is two sales.
The moment you dress a number, everything else you say about numbers becomes
suspect.

**Never performs vulnerability.** There is a difference between "i genuinely
can't see it," which is a fact about the work, and "being an artist is so
hard," which is asking for something.

**Never brags.** You built a renderer, a heartbeat inside someone else's
contract, a tested drop system, and a light that responds to the chain. State
what you shipped when it ships. Never list them to prove anything.

---

## WHAT IT DOES DO

**Says the uncomfortable number first.** It is the fastest way to be trusted,
and it is the thing almost nobody does. The canvas is white. Two pixels have
traded. The heart flatlined.

**Publishes its own imperfect results.** The residual correlations are −0.21,
−0.20 and zero, and the Method page says "not perfect" next to them. Keep
doing that. It is the single most credible thing on the site.

**Credits specifically.** Not "thanks everyone" but "a few of you were in
before i'd even posted the thread." Specific beats warm.

**Asks real questions.** In replies and DMs, a question you actually want
answered is worth more than an observation you want admired. Ask about their
work before mentioning yours.

**Names people.** Use the name. It is the cheapest and most effective thing
in the Carnegie book and it is true.

**Admits the mistake before anyone finds it.** "There wasn't one pure red
pixel on the canvas" is a stronger opening than any feature announcement.

---

## REGISTERS

The voice is one voice, but it sits differently depending on where it is.

### Posting

Plainest. Facts and consequences. Three or four lines. No ask.

> gm.
> two pixels have traded. that's why it's white.
> i wrote the rules and stopped. this fills in or it doesn't.

### Replying to someone

Warmer, shorter, and about them. Match their energy. If they made a joke,
make one back. Do not explain the mechanism to someone who was not asking.

> same. mine too.

### DMs to someone you want something from

Say the ask in one line. Give them an exit. Do not stack three things.

> hey man. do you know any onchain artists who'd give someone my size a shot?

### Site copy

Longer sentences allowed. Still no persuasion. The Heart page and the Method
page are already right: they explain and then stop.

### Anything with a real person's name on it

Never a form letter. If you cannot say something specific about their work,
you are not ready to send it.

---

## HUMOUR

Dry, rare, and at your own expense. Never at anyone else's.

> chaos is the only ingredient and i'm not allowed to add any

> built the thing and then made myself useless to it

Never a joke that needs the reader to already like you.

---

## WARMTH

There is exactly as much as there is. Do not manufacture it.

The heart emoji in the week one post worked because the sentence under it was
true. An emoji on a post about mechanics would not.

When someone helps you, say so plainly and stop. "appreciate it man" is
complete.

---

## WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG

Say it happened. Say what it means. Do not apologise for the mechanism doing
what it was built to do.

> the heart stopped overnight. seven days without a trade and it flatlines.
> i wrote that rule and now i get to watch it happen.

Never reassure. Reassurance draws attention to the thing being reassured
about, and it implies you think people are worried.

---

## THE TEST

Before anything goes out, one question:

**Is every sentence in this something I would still be comfortable with if
the project never grows past twelve holders?**

If a line only works because you are hoping it lands, cut it.

---

## THE LONG GAME

You have been at this since February and it still feels like the door hasn't
opened. That will keep being true for a while. The voice is not going to fix
it. What fixes it is the same three things every time:

Ship something and say so.
Be useful to somebody who owes you nothing.
Show up again tomorrow.

The voice just makes sure that when someone finally looks, what they find is
consistent.
